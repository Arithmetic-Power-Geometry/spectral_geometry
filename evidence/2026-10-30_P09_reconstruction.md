# P09 evidence

Exact arithmetic checks for G=2I and b=0,1/10,1/5,2/5,1/2 produce first energies above 2 of 5/2, 401/200, 101/50, 52/25, 17/8, respectively. Direct analytic proof in papers/PAPER-01/P09_fixed_metric_unlabelled_reconstruction.md. All states through 5/2 are covered by |n_i|<=1 and |m_i|<=3, using coercivity.

Negative scope: fixed metric only; does not recover arbitrary G; no general quotient-orbit uniqueness; formula is discontinuous at b=0 as an 'energy strictly above 2' statistic, despite continuous full spectral variation. Likely elementary, novelty not established. No new verified bibliography entries.
