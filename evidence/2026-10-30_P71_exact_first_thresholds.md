# P71 — Paper-2-style exact first-separation certificate
Date: 2026-10-30.
Scope: rectangular two-torus B=0, G=diag(A/r,Ar), A,r positive rational. 15 representatives (A<=r) from {1,4/3,3/2,2,3}. Full exact E<=4 charge enumeration with Fraction, certified finite box by coercivity.
For a background define coefficient multiset C(A,r)={A/r,r/A,Ar,1/(Ar)}. Its unrefined theta series is product over c in C of sum_{n in Z} q^{c n^2}. For two *different* coefficient multisets C,D, let delta be least positive rational c whose multiplicity in C differs from D. Then their energy-only theta coefficients agree for E<delta and differ by 2(m_C(delta)-m_D(delta)) at E=delta. Proof: factors of energy below delta match; at delta the only newly differing primitive contributions are +/-1 in factors of coefficient delta; any terms assembled from smaller factors agree because those factor multisets coincide. This is a standard elementary theta-product argument, not a new general Narain theorem. The result does not cover distinct models with identical coefficient multisets or general B!=0.
Executed exact P71 script checks all 105 pairs: first energy threshold equals delta, and first spin-refined threshold equals delta for every tested pair. Histogram: 1/9:14; 1/6:13; 2/9:12; 1/4:20; 1/3:17; 3/8:7; 4/9:7; 1/2:9; 9/16:3; 2/3:2; 3/4:1. Maximum threshold 3/4 for (A,r)=(1,1) vs (1,4/3). Spin has zero advantage in this sample, but no universal no-advantage theorem is claimed.
Novelty gate: exact finite-family proof is robust but elementary, likely insufficient as standalone publication. Paper 2 used exact threshold and a nontrivial spin discrepancy; this P71 does not reproduce the novel aspect of Paper 2. Next P72: adversarially seek pairs with equal coefficient multisets but distinct spin pairing, and extend to B!=0 where spin can separate earlier; do not claim novelty until compared with Paper 2 and prior literature.
