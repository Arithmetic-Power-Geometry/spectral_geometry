# P30 coordinate dependence of the proposed certificate invariant

Exact counterexample, independently executed with SymPy. Let phi(y0)=phi(y1)=t and D=y0-y1. D is one permitted equal-image binomial. Under invertible polynomial change z0=y0, z1=y0+y1, the same D=2z0-z1 and phi(z0)=t, phi(z1)=2t. D still lies in the kernel, but its two supported monomials no longer have equal images. Thus the fixed-support equal-image binomial certificate model is not preserved under arbitrary invertible linear changes of polynomial coordinates.

Scope: This disproves an automatic coordinate-invariance inference, not invariance under a smaller physically permitted equivalence group. It does not show that the published 454 value changes under a specific Narain duality. A physically meaningful invariant would require an explicitly specified physical equivalence group and proof of invariance under it.

The 454 optimum of P28-P29 remains valid in the chosen refined-enumerator coordinates. The primary genus-two distinction is known from Henriksson, Kakkar, McPeak, Narain CFTs and quantum codes at higher genus, JHEP 04 (2023) 011, DOI 10.1007/JHEP04(2023)011.
