# P10 gap audit

For fixed G=2I and b in (0,1/2], the tracked spectral energy is e=2+b*b/2. The inverse b=sqrt(2*(e-2)) is Holder-1/2 under energy error delta, with parameter error at most sqrt(2*delta). Exact gap checks: b=1/100 gives 1/20000; b=1/10 gives 1/200; b=1/5 gives 1/50; b=1/2 gives 1/8.

At b=0, the first level strictly above 2 is 5/2; as b approaches zero from above, that level approaches 2. Therefore this chosen level statistic is discontinuous at zero and noisy level identification is not uniformly robust. Unknown G invalidates the fixed-threshold formula. No new novelty established.
