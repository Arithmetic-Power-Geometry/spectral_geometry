Exact independently executed test: G=I2, B=0, energy E=||m||²+||n||², spin m dot n. All states with E<=2 enumerated using [-2,2]^4; 33 states. Genus-two diagonal-period-matrix theta kernel coefficient array factorizes into the product of the one-handle arrays. 1089 ordered state pairs, 25 distinct labels (E1,s1,E2,s2). One-handle coefficients: (0,0):1; (1,0):8; (2,-1):4; (2,0):16; (2,1):4.

Scope: This is a diagonal theta-kernel identity, not full genus-two CFT partition-function normalization. Full oscillator determinant, nonseparating degeneration, modular covariance, and known nontrivial isospectral benchmark remain unverified. No breakthrough or novel theorem claimed.
