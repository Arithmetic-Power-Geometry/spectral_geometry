# P39 exact separating-factorization kernel

Let R=Q[y0,...,y9], S=Q[x0,x1,x2,z0,z1,z2]. Let Phi:R->S map y0->x0z0, y1->x0z1, y2->x1z0, y3->x1z1, y4->x0z2, y5->x2z0, y6->x2z2, y7->x1z2, y8->x2z1, y9->x2z2. This is the P24 separating substitution, not the full genus-two theta specialization.

THEOREM. ker(Phi)=(y9-y6, all nine 2x2 minors of [[y0,y1,y4],[y2,y3,y7],[y5,y8,y6]]).

PROOF. Quotient by y9-y6 identifies the two duplicated variables, giving a polynomial ring in the nine entries of M. The induced map sends M_ij to x_i z_j. Its image is the affine cone over the Segre embedding P^2 x P^2, whose defining ideal is generated by the 2x2 minors of M (the standard rank-one determinantal ideal). Pull back this ideal to R. Thus exactly the displayed generators define ker(Phi). This also shows that the nine products x_i z_j are not algebraically independent: y0*y3-y1*y2, for example, lies in the kernel.

Independent local SymPy substitution checked all ten generators (one linear and nine minors) map to zero exactly. This computation checks inclusion; the standard determinantal theorem establishes completeness. Script available as local artifact /mnt/data/p39_factorization_kernel.py; not claimed GitHub-committed.

LIMITS: Kernel of the separating polynomial factorization is NOT the kernel of the full genus-two physical theta specialization. It cannot establish CFT equivalence, physical genus-two indistinguishability, or novel mathematics. The determinantal description is classical algebraic geometry. Future P40 should study actual theta-function identities and physically normalized coefficients using source equations.

Sources: Henriksson, Kakkar, McPeak, Narain CFTs and quantum codes at higher genus, JHEP 04 (2023) 011, DOI 10.1007/JHEP04(2023)011; Henriksson, Kakkar, McPeak, Classical codes and chiral CFTs at higher genus, JHEP 05 (2022) 159, DOI 10.1007/JHEP05(2022)159.
