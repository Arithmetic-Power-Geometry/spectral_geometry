## Construction and verified experiment
Two diagonal positive-definite backgrounds G=diag(2,c), B=0 with c=22/7 and c'=22/7+1/100000. Quantize individual charge-labelled energy E=m1²/2+m2²/c+2n1²+c n2² to nearest 0.01 using exact Fraction floor(x/q+1/2). Exhaustive charge box all 625 quadruples [-2,2]^4: 0 mismatches. Same for c'=c+1/1000000 and c'=c+1/10000000. Unrounded energies differ, e.g. n2=1. Executed /mnt/data/p93_precision_obstruction.py with PASS assertions.
## Exact duality inequivalence certificate
For diagonal G=diag(a,c), tau=i sqrt(c/a), rho=i sqrt(a*c). Here c/a>1, ac>1, so both are strict interiors of PSL2Z fundamental domain on imaginary axis. The unordered reduced moduli are determined by squared imaginary parts {c/a,ac}. The pairs for c and c' differ. Simultaneous reflection fixes imaginary-axis moduli; swap only exchanges pair. Worldsheet parity also fixes both because B=0. Therefore the two backgrounds are inequivalent under these modular, swap, reflection and parity operations. This is a stronger control than the sign-B example.
Primary source: Hosono, Lian, Oguiso, Yau, c=2 Rational Toroidal Conformal Field Theories via the Gauss Product, Commun Math Phys 241 (2003) 245–286, arXiv hep-th/0211230, proposition 2.7 and Appendix C, https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/58/489-017-0211230.pdf .
## Important limitations
Charge-labelled fixed-coordinate box is not itself duality-invariant, although exact equality of the entire tested labelled table implies equality of its unlabelled rounded energy multiset. The latter is a finite *charge cutoff*, not a physically invariant energy cutoff. For this specific pair, a sufficiently large energy cutoff or finer resolution separates the theories. The general fact that finitely many continuous measurements at fixed finite precision cannot separate all points in a continuous family is elementary continuity, not novel. Do not claim a new inverse-spectral theorem or universal impossibility for every conceivable adaptive observation.
