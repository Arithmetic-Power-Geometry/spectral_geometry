# P22 exact symmetry quotient; not yet a physical theta substitution

Independent execution: source P19 published graph-code matrices and complete 16-type genus-two joint enumerators. Each ordered pair of 2-bit symbols (4x4=16) is mapped to its unordered orbit under swapping the two handles. There are 4 diagonal and 6 off-diagonal orbits, totaling 10. For each original 16-entry exponent vector, aggregate exponents within these orbits, preserving integer coefficient multiplicities. This is a well-defined polynomial substitution, not the physical theta map.

Results: projected polynomial 1 has 1432 nonzero monomials; polynomial 2 has 1395; 1636 exponent labels have unequal coefficients; both retain total multiplicity 16384. First differing lexicographic exponent (0,0,0,0,0,0,1,0,5,1). Exact Python assertions passed.

Critical caveat: ten unordered joint-type variables do not automatically coincide with the ten genus-two theta functions in Henriksson, Kakkar, McPeak, JHEP 04 (2023) 011, DOI 10.1007/JHEP04(2023)011. Physical theta substitution, modular transformation laws and oscillator normalization are NOT derived. Nonzero formal difference need not imply nonzero physically normalized Fourier coefficient.

Primary publisher page verified: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP04(2023)011 . Prior art: paper already proves genus-two separation of genus-one-isospectral code CFTs. No novelty established.
