# P125: exact full-interval deterministic adaptive-versus-fixed gap

Date: 2026-11-01. Scope: five-qubit [[5,1,3]] stabilizer generated by XZZXI, IXZZX, XIXZZ, ZXIXZ; ideal independent site I/Z dephasing with a shared scalar t in [0,1].

Let f(t)=1-10t^3+15t^4-6t^5. P124's exact 16-syndrome certificate establishes P_ML(t)=f(t) for t in [0,1/2]. Since ZZZZZ commutes with each stabilizer generator but is not a stabilizer, multiplication by ZZZZZ swaps the two supported logical cosets in each syndrome. Thus P_ML(t)=P_ML(1-t), giving P_ML(t)=f(min(t,1-t)) over [0,1]. Hence min_t P_ML(t)=f(1/2)=1/2.

At t=0, only IIIII occurs; at t=1, only ZZZZZ occurs. Both have syndrome 0000 but inhabit distinct stabilizer cosets. Any single deterministic syndrome decoder must fail at one endpoint. Therefore max_D min_{t in [0,1]} P_D(t)=0, whereas min_t max_D P_D(t)=1/2. Adaptivity gap for this specific model: 1/2.

Assumes perfect syndrome extraction and deterministic fixed decoders, with extreme dephasing endpoints allowed; randomized decoders fall outside the stated model.
