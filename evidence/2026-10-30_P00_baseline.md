# P00 baseline and caution ledger — 2026-10-30

Evidence type: manuscript-derived programme initialization and initial external publisher checks. Not a comprehensive prior-art audit.

## Positive starting points
Five charge-labelled measurements suffice in a fixed d=2 basis; exact T(b) for one rational metric; certified finite charge-box methodology.

## Negative / falsification controls
- Five measurements have **not** been proven minimal.
- B to -B parity is not a new physical non-identifiability mechanism.
- Spin disagreement does not demonstrate inequivalence of unoriented theories.
- Graph coarse enumerators in source are not invariant under local complementation.
- Numerically distinguished genus-two combinatorial refinements are not automatically physically normalized partition functions.
- Existing splitting results use classical Kneser-type ingredients, so cannot be claimed as novel general algorithms.
- No claim about practical RSA factoring, ML-KEM breaking or quantum error-correction superiority follows.
