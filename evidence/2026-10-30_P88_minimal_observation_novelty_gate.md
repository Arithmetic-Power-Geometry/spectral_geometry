# P88 — Novelty gate for minimal physically distinguishing observations
Date: 2026-10-30
## Question
For explicitly defined Narain CFT family F modulo physically correct equivalence, what minimal finite collection of physically measurable, normalized observables separates a chosen inequivalent pair, and can minimality be proved? This is a candidate question, NOT a theorem.
## Verified prior art and collision risks
1. Henriksson, Kakkar, McPeak (2023), Narain CFTs and quantum codes at higher genus, JHEP 04 (2023) 011, DOI 10.1007/JHEP04(2023)011. Already constructs genus-one-isospectral but genus-two-distinct Narain code CFTs. In P87 our original pair matched its seven-vertex example under ELC/relabeling.
2. Henriksson, Kakkar, McPeak (2022), Classical codes and chiral CFTs at higher genus, JHEP 05 (2022) 159, DOI 10.1007/JHEP05(2022)159. Higher-genus constraints and separating degeneration for chiral code CFTs; excludes claims of generic new genus hierarchy.
3. Furuta (2022), Relation between spectra of Narain CFTs and properties of associated boolean functions, JHEP 09 (2022) 146, DOI 10.1007/JHEP09(2022)146. Explicit connection to Boolean functions, creating direct novelty collision for transferring Boolean invariants to Narain spectra.
4. Yahagi (2022), Narain CFTs and error-correcting codes on finite fields, JHEP 08 (2022) 058, DOI 10.1007/JHEP08(2022)058. Code enumerators, spectral gaps and Narain lattices over odd prime fields.
5. Henriksson & McPeak (2023), Averaging over codes and an SU(2) modular bootstrap, JHEP 11 (2023) 035, DOI 10.1007/JHEP11(2023)035. Additional higher-genus ensemble and modular constraints.
## Operational definition and falsification gates
Distinguish: (i) minimal genus of complete partition functions, (ii) minimal q-expansion cutoff at fixed genus, (iii) minimal number of chosen numerical period-matrix evaluations, (iv) minimal spin/charge-labelled observations. These are different optimization problems and must never be conflated. Need fixed family, allowed probes, exact duality equivalence, normalization, and physical attainability.
A generic observation 'find first distinguishing coefficient' on known HKM pair is likely a benchmark calculation, not novel. A universal finite cutoff over unrestricted Narain moduli is false under earlier noncompactness obstructions. A claim of a novel Boolean-to-Narain bridge requires kill test against Furuta 2022.
## Recommended research route
P89 choose restricted rational two-torus family with explicit G,B and finite bounded denominator/height, define a computable charge/spin observation budget and exact O(2,2;Z) duality canonicalization. Then prove or disprove finite reconstruction under bounded height with explicit optimality witnesses. Alternatively search beyond known code CFT pair for an original minimal genus-two coefficient certificate. These are candidates pending source audit.
## Decision
P88 REVISE: problem taxonomy and stronger prior-art novelty gate established; no original theorem or new physical test. Paper 4 NO-GO.
