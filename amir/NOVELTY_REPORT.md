# Stage 02 — novelty and research-positioning report

**Status: PARTIAL / UNRESOLVED.** Targeted sources checked; exhaustive mathematical priority search not completed. This report is a research note, not manuscript prose or a journal eligibility declaration.

## Findings supported by inspected literature
- The existence/rationality of Poincaré series counting polynomial congruence solutions is a classical topic in Igusa and Denef's theory. A claim of novelty solely from obtaining a rational function would be unsound.
- Explicit formulas for p-adic local zeta functions are an established area (Denef–Hoornaert 2001). The paper should identify the **specific** numerator, primitive-pair split, capped valuation counts and strict-mask application rather than claim a novel rationality paradigm.
- Quasipolynomial counts and explicit periodic coefficients are well studied in lattice-point enumeration (Beck–Robins and related work). The modular window statistic may be specialized, but generic quasipolynomial behavior is not original.
- CRT and inclusion–exclusion formulas should be described as exact applications, not new CRT principles.

## Tentative strongest contributions (not yet independently established as original)
1. Explicit joint valuation distribution of (x−y, x²+xy+y²) for p>3, with a precise closed numerator.
2. Closed finite-sum strict prime-power masking K_p(e) and equivalence to coefficient extraction.
3. Specialized exact bounded-window coefficient formulas for semiprimes and square-free moduli, including an exact first-occurrence threshold in a restricted prime family.

## Weakest novelty claims
Elementary four-state classification; root-of-unity lifting; CRT product counts; generic rationality; generic quasipolynomiality.

## Mandatory next research checks
- Search for formulas explicitly treating v_p(x−y), v_p(x²+xy+y²), the resultant of these two factors, and x³≡y³ mod p^e.
- Compare the precise strict-mask statistic (neither factor individually divisible by p^e) with existing polynomial-congruence counting.
- Derive K_p(T) and recurrence rigorously; verify the result is more than a mechanical corollary of known series rationality.
- Separate true prior-art matches from merely related general frameworks; do not infer novelty from a failed keyword search.

## Editorial positioning
Suggested cautious statement for later *author-independent* redrafting: “We give explicit formulas for a specified cubic-factor masking statistic at prime powers and in bounded windows, using local valuation distributions and CRT lifting.” Avoid “first,” “new theory,” “cryptographic breakthrough,” or “efficient factorization.”
