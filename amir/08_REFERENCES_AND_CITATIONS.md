5. Ensure each numbered table/figure/algorithm is introduced, called out and interpreted in surrounding prose. Verify labels, equation/theorem cross-references, numbering and every appendix mention.
6. Do not imply the provided examples or verification scripts were independently published unless they have actually been archived.
