## Formal search state
A research node is S=(D,H,E,C,P), where D is a list of precise definitions and admissible equivalences, H is proved statements and explicitly unproved conjectures, E is executable evidence with scope and exact provenance, C is known counterexamples and falsification controls, and P is verified prior art. A candidate move is an explicit conjecture or reduction with assumptions and predicted new consequences. A move is admissible only when it does not silently change D, and it records proof obligations.

## Chess analogues
Candidate move generation: alternative mathematical questions, restrictions and strengthened hypotheses.
Depth search: attempt consequences, counterexamples, prior-art reductions and exact test cases before committing.
Opponent model: construct strongest counterexample; compare against best known theorem.
Quiescence test: do not claim a theorem while definitions, normalization, duality quotient, numerical completeness or published conventions are unstable.
Transposition: detect equivalent reformulations and repeated results; reject rebranding.
Evaluation: mathematical correctness and independent novelty are hard gates; computational tractability and physics meaning are further hard gates; elegance and breadth are soft priorities.
Proof completion: each inference justified, assumptions explicit, independent checking passed, literature comparison passed.
