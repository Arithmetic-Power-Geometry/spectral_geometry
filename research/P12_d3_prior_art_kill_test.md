# P12: d>=3 nonproduct Narain research gate (2026-10-30)

## Research question
Does the unlabelled genus-one zero-mode theta series determine a generic genuinely nonproduct d=3 Narain background up to O(3,3;Z), parity, and stated equivalences? This is a QUESTION, not a theorem.

## Prior-art barriers
Henriksson, Kakkar and McPeak, JHEP 04 (2023) 011, DOI 10.1007/JHEP04(2023)011, exhibit genus-one-isospectral code CFTs with differing genus-two partition functions. Therefore broad Narain spectral injectivity claims are false in some code-CFT settings; determine dimensions and assumptions before importing results to d=3.

Yahagi, JHEP 08 (2022) 058, DOI 10.1007/JHEP08(2022)058, constructs Narain CFTs from codes over finite fields; code constructions are an essential competing method.

Angelinos, Chakraborty, Dymarsky, JHEP 11 (2022) 118, DOI 10.1007/JHEP11(2022)118, studies optimal Narain CFTs from codes. Its spectral-gap constructions are not evidence for a new inverse-theta theorem.

Chu and Jung, arXiv:2610.22633 (2026), study sharp Narain gap bounds at c=2,3 using exceptional isomorphisms. This is a current preprint and a direct reason not to assert novelty from d=3 or spectral-gap calculations alone. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22633 . Publication status and detailed theorems require separate audit.

## Explicit mathematical controls
G is symmetric positive-definite 3x3 (6 real parameters); B antisymmetric 3x3 (3 real parameters). Dimension of moduli before quotient: 9. Charge (m,n) in Z^3 x Z^3, E=(m+Bn)^T G^-1(m+Bn)+n^T G n. For finite H and eigenvalue bounds mu<=eig(G)<=M and ||B||_2<=beta, ||n||<=sqrt(H/mu), ||m||<=sqrt(HM)+beta sqrt(H/mu).

Nonproduct condition cannot mean merely G has off-diagonal entries: integral basis changes may disguise a product. Require an invariant obstruction to integral orthogonal splitting and duality, or label cases only 'off-diagonal in chosen basis'.

## Strict falsification-first sequence
(1) Specify generic/measure and quotient; (2) build independent exact-rational d=3 charge enumerator and completeness proof; (3) generate off-diagonal examples, test trivial dualities and parity; (4) compare exact fingerprints at growing cutoff; (5) classify any putative full-spectrum collision under O(3,3;Z) with proof; (6) require theorem-level novelty audit before writing.
