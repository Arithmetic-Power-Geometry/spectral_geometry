# P74 — Observable pivot: symmetry-twined Narain spectra (proposal and hostile gate)
Date: 2026-10-30

## Why change observable
P69–P73 energy/spin experiments either gave elementary finite-window facts or reproduced Paper 2's B-sign involution. Continuing to expand the same grid is unlikely to provide independent novelty.

## Candidate physical observable
For an actual CFT symmetry g commuting with the Hamiltonian, Z_g(tau,bar tau)=Tr_H[g q^(L0-c/24) bar q^(L0bar-cbar/24)]. At charge level, for a symmetry acting diagonally on states, one may consider weighted multiplicities M_g(E,s)=sum_{states at E,s} character_g(state). If g permutes charge states, the trace must use the representation action on the energy eigenspace; it is NOT generally a naive sign weighting of all charge labels.

## Immediate obstruction and literature
Symmetry-twined and twisted partition functions are classical orbifold CFT objects. In particular modular transformations mix (g,h) sectors rather than preserving an isolated Z_g. Any proposed physical invariant must specify an actual symmetry of each background, action on oscillators, fixed-point/twisted sectors as relevant, modular covariance, and equivalence of symmetry labels across two CFTs. Comparing arbitrary coordinate parity operators across inequivalent models is not meaningful.
Background-specific generic isometries and B-field compatibility must be checked; no assumption that a selected coordinate reflection is a symmetry when B!=0. Also the canonical global charge inversion (m,n)->(-m,-n) is ubiquitous and may give a vacuous twining with no novel distinguishing power.

## Candidate precise research questions
H74-A: On a stated finite rational Narain family with a *common physically valid symmetry group*, find and prove a minimal collection of twined partition functions distinguishing specified inequivalent CFTs. Distinguish minimality on a finite dataset (combinatorial set cover, generally known) from a new structural theorem.
H74-B: Characterize exact obstructions to transporting a chosen symmetry-twining observable across duality orbits; require equivariant duality conjugacy. Danger: standard equivariant CFT/orbifold theory.
H74-C: Exhibit a parameterized family with provable unrefined/spin-isospectrality and a symmetry-resolved first discrepancy, not reducible to Paper 2 or known code-CFT results. No witness presently.

## P75 executable research gate
1. Fix one explicit Narain background with nontrivial exact symmetry and write its action on charges and oscillator modes.
2. Check Q and spin invariance symbolically, then compute trace coefficients by exact orbit/permutation counting.
3. Independently check modular covariance using known (g,h) sectors before making physical statements.
4. Search for a pair of duality-inequivalent models with matching ordinary spectra but different symmetry-resolved spectra. Prove equivalence notion and first threshold.
5. Kill-test against orbifold CFT, lattice theta with characteristics, code-CFT weight enumerators, and prior three manuscripts.

No new theorem is claimed in P74. This is a research design and literature-risk assessment, not a completed physical computation.
