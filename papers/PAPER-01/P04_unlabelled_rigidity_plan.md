# P04 — Unlabelled inverse spectral rigidity: falsification-first protocol

Observation: multiset {E_G,B(m,n) : (m,n) in Z^4} with multiplicities, zero-mode convention fixed. Compare only after stating fixed basis, O(2,2;Z) equivalence, and whether worldsheet parity is included.

**Do not claim generic injectivity**: E_G,B(m,n)=E_G,-B(-m,n) universally. Therefore energy-only data cannot identify oriented B sign, even with an infinite exact spectrum, unless the equivalence quotient explicitly identifies parity.

Candidate C4-A (UNPROVED): within a rigorously specified reduced moduli subfamily quotienting parity and integral duality, is the full unlabelled spectrum generically injective? Need concrete family and proof or exact counterexample.
Candidate C4-B (UNPROVED): on compact semialgebraic rational parameter boxes with a noncollision margin, is there a computable finite energy cutoff identifying a finite set of candidate moduli? Beware continuous families and collisions.
Candidate C4-C (UNPROVED): characterize local walls where first N energy levels change ordering; construct exact semialgebraic chamber decomposition, prove completeness with charge bounds; test whether this is more than standard quadratic-form reduction.
