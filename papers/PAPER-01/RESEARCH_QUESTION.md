# PAPER-01 — Research proposal, not manuscript

Working title: Minimal Spectral Data for Reconstruction of Narain Geometry.

Observation hierarchy: arbitrary fixed charge-labelled evaluations; adaptively chosen labels; unlabelled multiplicity spectra; spin-resolved spectra. Explicitly distinguish exact vs noisy data and fixed-basis vs O(d,d;Z)-orbit recovery.

First candidate: Find a nontrivial and sharp minimal number of admissible charge-labelled probes for generic versus all d=2 backgrounds; produce matching lower and constructive upper bounds. Existing five-probe inversion must serve as baseline, not be rebranded as novel. Compare necessary dimension arguments with topology and possible nonlinear/adaptive identifiability. Search for counterexamples under different probe constraints. Publication gate: full proof and strongest-prior-art novelty verification.
